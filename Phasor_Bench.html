Save, load, export

The circuit as text you can keep in a file, or as a SPICE netlist for PSpice, LTspice or ngspice.

Sine sources are entered as RMS, other waveforms as peak value; every phasor is the RMS value of one harmonic. A passive part counts U and I from its first terminal to its second, in the direction of the small arrow on its lead. A source shows what it delivers. Values accept prefixes and a decimal comma: 4,7k · 100n · 2.2u · 15m. Drag a panel by the dotted grip in its top corner to move it, and by the hatched corner at its bottom right to resize it; the bar between the two columns can be dragged as well. The circuit, the theme and the panel arrangement are kept in this browser.
